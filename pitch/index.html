The new leak — since 2025

The questions now go to an AI that doesn't know the course

88% of students already use AI to learn — only 15% say it's integrated in their courses.

The AI answers without the teacher's notes or the group — and nothing comes back to either.

Used loose, it hurts: students practicing with a generic chatbot scored 17% worse on the AI-free exam.

The solution

Ada gives the course a shared memory

Agents are members. The teacher creates them and adds them to channels, like teaching assistants — with a name and a face, not a "bot".

Talk becomes cards. What gets understood compiles into typed, versioned pages with sources — outside the chat flow.

The group owns the files. Plain markdown the course can list, read and take with it — not rows in a vendor's database.
